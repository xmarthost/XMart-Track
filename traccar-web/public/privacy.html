XMart Track – Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

XMart Track is a vehicle GPS tracking service (website and Android/iPhone app). This policy explains which data the service processes, why, and the choices you have.

Data we process

  • Account data: your name, login (email) and password (stored encrypted/hashed).
  • Vehicle data: locations, speed, direction, ignition (ACC) state, battery voltage and other values sent by the GPS tracker installed in the vehicle, and the tracker's IMEI and SIM number.
  • Alert settings: phone numbers you enter for SMS and WhatsApp alerts, geofences and alert choices.
  • App data: a push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so the server can send alerts to your phone, and app logs kept only on your phone for troubleshooting.

The app does not collect your phone's own location, contacts, photos or files, and shows no ads.

Why we use it

  • To show your vehicles on the map, their history (playback), reports and addresses.
  • To send the alerts you switched on (app notification, WhatsApp, SMS), e.g. ignition on/off, overspeed, geofence.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

Sharing

Data is not sold. It is shared only with services needed to provide the features you use:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (app notifications).
  • The WhatsApp/SMS gateway used for alerts (only the alert text and the receiving number).
  • Map, address (geocoding) and road-matching services, which receive map positions to draw maps, addresses and the route line.

Vehicle data is visible to the account owner and to the users the owner shares the vehicles with.

Security and storage

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on the XMart Track server. Location history is kept for the period set by the fleet owner and then deleted.

Your choices and account deletion

  • You can change your details and alert numbers in the app (Me → Account) or on the website.
  • You can delete your account in the app: Me → Account, password & delete account → Delete my account, or on the web: delete your account.
  • Deleting the account removes your login, settings, alert numbers and push tokens. Vehicles and their location history belong to the fleet owner who added them and are kept or deleted by that owner.

Children

The service is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact
